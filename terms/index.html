2. What the Service is

CalledIt is a price-discovery copilot. You ask about a game in chat; we compare prices across bookmakers, betting exchanges and prediction markets, and tell you where the best one is.

There are two ways a bet then happens, and the difference matters:

  • Handoff. For most venues we give you a link. You open it and place the bet yourself, in your own account, with that venue. We are not involved in the transaction.
  • Connected venue. If you choose to connect a venue that we support directly (currently Cloudbet), you give us an API key issued by your own account with that venue. We then place bets on that account when, and only when, you tap the Place button on a specific quote.

We hold no money, ever. There is no CalledIt wallet, no CalledIt balance, and no deposit you can make to us. Your stake is your own balance at your own venue, and it never passes through us. Nothing on our books represents your money.
