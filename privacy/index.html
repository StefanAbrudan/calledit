What CalledIt is, in one line

CalledIt compares betting prices and, if you connect your own account at a venue, places bets on that account when you tap Place. We never hold your money and we take no payments, so we hold no card, bank, or identity-document data at all. That shapes everything below.

Data we collect

  • Your chat identity. On Telegram, your Telegram user id and, if you have one, your username. On the web, the identifier you sign in with through our authentication provider Privy (Telegram or email) and your Privy user id.
  • Your settings. The country you tell us, the fact and time that you confirmed you are 18 or over, your per-bet and daily caps, your staking currency, and whether you have paused placement.
  • A venue API key, if you connect one. Stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM, together with the venue name and whether the last call using it succeeded. On Telegram we delete the message containing the key once it is stored.
  • Your bets. Bets we place for you and bets you tell us you placed elsewhere: the selection, stake, price, currency, venue, timestamps, and the result once settled.
  • Your messages. What you write to the bot, so it can answer. See the next section.
  • Technical data. Server logs and basic device or browser information, kept for security and debugging.

Your messages and the language model

To understand a question like "arsenal v coventry tonight, best price on arsenal?", the bot sends your message, and recent turns of the conversation for context, to a language model operated by Google (Gemini). Google processes it to generate the reply. Please do not send the bot anything sensitive that you would not want processed this way. The bot is built to answer questions about markets and prices, and nothing more.

What we deliberately do not collect

We take no payments, so there is no card number, bank detail, or billing address anywhere in our systems. We run no identity verification, so we hold no passport, ID, or proof of address. We create no wallet for you and hold no balance. Any identity checks and any funding of your account happen at your venue, under its own policies, with no involvement from us.

Why we process your data

  • To provide the service (answer your questions, quote prices, place a bet you tapped, show your bets and results): performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To keep placement lawful and safe (the 18+ confirmation, the country check a venue requires, caps and pause): legal obligation and legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(c) and 6(1)(f).
  • Security and abuse prevention (rate limiting, logs): legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f).
  • Legal obligations (accounting, responding to lawful requests): Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Some providers process data outside the EEA, including in the United States; transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

Retention and deletion

Send disconnect and your stored venue API key is deleted. Ask us at the address above to delete your account and we remove your settings, your key, and your bet records, keeping only what accounting or other law requires. You can revoke an API key directly in your venue account at any time, which takes effect immediately whether or not you tell us. Server logs are kept briefly for security.
